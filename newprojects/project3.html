Kiatnakin Phatra New Years' Gift Collection 2026

For Kiatnakin Phatra Financial Group’s New Year’s gift collection, I designed the key artwork and applied the visual concept across a range of gift items. The design draws inspiration from Yantra, a traditional Thai art form rooted in faith, symbolism, and Buddhist philosophy. Composed of flowing lines and geometric structures, the artwork reinterprets the spiritual language of Yantra into a contemporary visual expression. What may appear as abstract lines at first gradually reveals a sense of rhythm and intention, reflecting themes of guidance, protection, and prosperity.
