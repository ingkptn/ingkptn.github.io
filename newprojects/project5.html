Little Canal: Cafe Rebrand

Rebrand of Little Canal, a neighborhood coffee shop and bar in New York City’s Chinatown. The new identity centers on community, celebrating the spirit of the neighborhood and the connections formed within it. Bold visual language and tactile design elements channel the raw, street-level energy of the area, creating a brand presence that feels authentic, welcoming, and distinctly tied to its surroundings.
