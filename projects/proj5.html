Historic Monuments of Ancient Kyoto

BRANDING & IDENTITY / GRAPHIC DESIGN / PUBLICATION
Project Type: University Coursework
Software(s): Adobe Illustrator, InDesign

Hypothetical Identity and Editorial Design for the UNESCO World Heritage Site: "Historic Monuments of Ancient Kyoto: Kyoto, Uji, and Otsu Cities”. This project explores the creation of a comprehensive visual identity and editorial system for one of Japan’s most culturally significant UNESCO World Heritage sites. The design initiative seeks to balance historical reverence with contemporary design sensibilities, creating a bridge between centuries-old heritage and modern forms of communication.
