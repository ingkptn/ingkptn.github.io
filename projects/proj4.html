KKP Chinese New Year 2026

GRAPHIC DESIGN / PACKAGING DESIGN / MOTION DESIGN
Project Type: Freelance
Software(s): Adobe Illustrator, AfterEffects

A contemporary interpretation of traditional Chinese New Year red packets, designed for Kiatnakin Phatra Bank. The collection explores themes of prosperity, continuity, and good fortune through abstracted auspicious forms, expressed as bold silhouettes and layered details. Created as a seasonal gesture for clients, the packets are intended to convey well wishes, appreciation, and a sense of lasting success.
