Work / Luck for Sale: The Economy of Belief

(2025)

Luck for Sale: The Economy of Belief (Parsons CD Senior Thesis)

Publication Design / Branding & Identity / Graphic Design
Project Type: University Coursework
Software(s): Adobe Illustrator, InDesign

As convenience and efficiency have become the main factors driving modern lifestyles, even deeply personal and spiritual practices have adapted to fit the fast-paced rhythm of urbanized societies. This project explores and provides a critical commentary on the intersection of belief systems, material culture, and capitalism in contemporary Thailand through a design-driven lens.
