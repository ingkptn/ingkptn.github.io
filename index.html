(2025)

Yantra Reverie (KKP New Year 2026)

GRAPHIC DESIGN / ILLUSTRATION
Project Type: Freelance
Software(s): Adobe Illustrator, AfterEffects

For Kiatnakin Phatra Financial Group’s New Year’s gift collection, I designed the key artwork and applied the visual concept across a range of gift items. The design draws inspiration from Yantra, a traditional Thai art form rooted in faith, symbolism, and Buddhist philosophy. Composed of flowing lines and geometric structures, the artwork reinterprets the spiritual language of Yantra into a contemporary visual expression. What may appear as abstract lines at first gradually reveals a sense of rhythm and intention, reflecting themes of guidance, protection, and prosperity.

(2024)

Block Mono

Typeface Design / Graphic Design
Project Type: University Coursework
Software(s): Adobe Illustrator, AfterEffects

Block Mono is a contemporary monospaced sans-serif typeface that draws inspiration from industrial design, architecture, and construction, subtly infused with a touch of calligraphic flair. Designed for versatility, each character is crafted with attention to proportion and form, balancing sharp, geometric precision with softened, rounded corners. This interplay creates a rhythm that feels both structured and approachable.

(2024)

256: Unpacking the Human Experience

PUBLICATION
Project Type: University Coursework
Software(s): Adobe InDesign

A series of 4 short booklets, in total containing 256 images, exploring the dimensions of the human experience that constitutes one's "reality" and their sense of self. Each booklet loosely investigates a different dimension— the physical, the relational, the temporal, and the mental-through the examination and organization of both representational and metaphorical imagery. Within each dimension, images are sorted into smaller, specific sub-categories. Images range from photographs to graphic design, paintings and installations.
